Support

Artsy for Mac, iPad and iPhone

Questions, problems and ideas go to support@artsydraw.com. Say which device and which version of Artsy (Settings shows it), and what you were doing; a screenshot or the diagnostics log helps.

Which devices run it

Macs with Apple silicon, iPads with the Apple Pencil, and iPhones. One purchase covers all three. On the Mac the window can be sized to the work; on iPad and iPhone the chrome folds away with a tap on Hide and the canvas fills the screen.

Where documents live

On iPad and iPhone, documents are kept in the Files app, in iCloud Drive or On My iPad, wherever you choose when you save. On the Mac they are ordinary files you save where you like. The format is the same on both, so a painting started on the iPad opens on the Mac from iCloud Drive and comes back the same way. A document holds its layers, its brush settings and the time-lapse of its painting.

The Apple Pencil

Pressure and tilt come from the Pencil and shape every stroke: the pencils press harder and lean flatter, the brushes widen, the watercolour pools. A finger, or a Pencil with no pressure sensor such as the USB-C model, still draws with pressure: Artsy takes it from the speed of the stroke, so a slow, deliberate line is heavy and a quick one is light. The pressure curve and the smoothing are in Settings.

AI generation and stock photos

The Add panel can generate an image with OpenAI or Google, or search Unsplash for a photo, each with a key of your own entered in Settings. Without a key the panel says so and everything else works as before. Your keys stay in the system Keychain on your device and go only to the service each belongs to; what you send, and when, is in the privacy policy.

The diagnostics log

Artsy keeps a log of what it does on your device, which helps with a report. On the Mac it is under Help ▸ Diagnostics ▸ Show Diagnostics Folder, and Export Diagnostics… packs it for sending. On iPad and iPhone it is in Settings under Diagnostics. The log never leaves your device on its own.

Something is wrong

  • A stroke feels too light or too heavy: look at the pressure curve in Settings, and tell us which Pencil you have.
  • A document will not open: say where it is kept and send the diagnostics log; the document itself helps most if you can share it.
  • The app is slow on a large canvas: say the canvas size and the brush; the log records the frame times.

Everything goes to support@artsydraw.com.
