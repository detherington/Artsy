A sketchbook that paints like the real thing.

Pencils in every grade from 2H to 6B. Watercolour that runs with the lean of the paper. Oils and acrylics that keep their bristle marks and their thickness. Every brush is a living mark, not a stamp.

The Pencil in every stroke

Pressure and tilt from the Apple Pencil, or pressure from speed with a finger or a Pencil that has none. The pencils answer to how you hold them and how fast you move.

Paint that behaves

Watercolour that stays wet and runs where you tilt the paper. Oils and acrylics with thickness you can see and bristle marks that stay. Pastel, chalk, conté, ink.

A studio, not a toy

Layers with blend modes, undo without limit, symmetry and guides, a colour panel built for a pen, and a time-lapse of every painting. Documents live in Files and iCloud Drive, in a format the Mac and the iPad share.

Artsy runs on Macs with Apple silicon, on iPad with the Apple Pencil, and on iPhone. AI generation and stock photos are there if you bring your own keys, and nothing leaves your device unless you ask. How Artsy treats your work.
