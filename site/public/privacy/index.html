Privacy policy

3 November 2026

Artsy is a sketching and painting app for Mac, iPad and iPhone. It keeps what you make on your device, and in your own iCloud Drive if you put it there.

What Artsy collects

Nothing. The app has no account, no analytics and no advertising, and it sends nothing about you or your work to its developer.

What stays on your device

Your documents, your brushes and your settings. On iPad and iPhone, documents are kept where you choose in the Files app, which may be iCloud Drive; that storage is Apple's and covered by Apple's privacy terms.

What leaves your device, and only if you ask

Two optional features send data to a third party, each with a key you supply in Settings:

  • AI generation sends your prompt, and the picture on the canvas if you choose "Generate from Canvas", to OpenAI or to Google, under your own API key and their terms. Nothing is sent until you tap Generate.
  • Stock images searches Unsplash with the words you type and downloads the photos you pick, under your own Unsplash access key and Unsplash's terms.

Your keys are kept in the system Keychain on your device and are sent only to the service each belongs to.

Diagnostics

The app writes a diagnostics log on your device, which you can look at or share from the app; it is never sent anywhere on its own.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it.
