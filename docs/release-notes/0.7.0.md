## Artsy 0.7.0 — the drawing engine, first class

Everything since 0.6.1 is the engine upgrade planned in `docs/drawing-engine-plan.md`.

### Drawing
- Strokes settle as they are drawn, so a frame costs the same however long the stroke is; smoothing catches up to the pen at lift-off; mouse strokes ease in and out.
- Stamp brushes: dabs with tips, grain, scatter, jitter, a second masking tip; build-up and wash accumulation; a resting airbrush keeps spraying.
- Wet media: smudge (dulling and smearing), pigment mixing (Kubelka–Munk, so yellow over blue makes green), watercolour washes with wet edges and granulation, thick paint (impasto) lit on the canvas and in exports, carried through moves, transforms, merges, undo and the file.
- Hold the pen still at the end of a stroke to snap it to a line, circle, ellipse, rectangle or polygon; guides and a grid to snap to (View ▸ Guides).
- Calligraphy nibs, velocity and tilt dynamics, per-pen pressure curves.

### Canvas and files
- Canvases up to 8192 × 8192 with up to 32 layers, within the GPU's memory; undo history within a memory cap, copying only what each step changes.
- 16-bit PNG export; everything tagged Display P3.
- Documents save atomically and keep thickness and guides; older files open as they were.
- Brush Studio for every setting; Photoshop `.abr` and Procreate `.brush`/`.brushset` import, checked against real files (names, settings, dual brushes).

### Fixed
- A review pass over the engine: undo could resurrect an undone stroke after a layer was deleted and restored; a bucket fill could overwrite strokes made while it ran; opening a document dropped its thickness and guides; saving mid-transform lost the transform; keys and undo were live mid-stroke; layer locks, blend modes in merges, and colour tagging for the clipboard and pastes.

### For testing
- Help ▸ Diagnostics: a log of what the app did and what the pen reported, stroke recording (on by default in this build), and Export Diagnostics… to gather it all into one folder to send back.
